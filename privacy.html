Privacy Policy

Study Guides: Bible Books • Effective November 17, 2026

Study Guides: Bible Books (“the app”) is a ministry of The Creed of Ink, Inc. (Creed of Ink Inc.), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Cartersville, Georgia, USA (“we”). This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

We do not collect your data

The app does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not sell or share user data, because we do not receive it.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, notes, reading plan, memorized verses, translation choice, and appearance settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server.

Backup

If you export a backup, the app creates a file on your device. That file goes only where you choose to save or share it; we never receive it. Importing a backup reads a file you select on your device. Clearing progress in Backup & settings, or uninstalling the app, removes the on-device data.

On Android, the app allows the system’s Auto Backup feature, so your own Google account may save app data if Android Backup is turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Android settings. We cannot access it. You can turn it off in Android Settings.

Bible text and chapter links

Quoted verses are bundled with the app and are read on your device; looking them up sends nothing to us. If you tap a link to read a full chapter, your device opens BibleGateway in your system browser. That visit is between you and BibleGateway and is covered by their privacy policy. The app does not send your notes or progress with that link.

Payments

The app is free, with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Optional donations to The Creed of Ink are made on our website or by text and are not processed inside the app.

Children

The app is rated for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date above.
