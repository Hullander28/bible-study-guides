tagline: Stand firm until the Lord comes, and do not grow weary in doing good
author: The apostle Paul, with Silvanus and Timothy (1:1).
date: About AD 51, from Corinth, a few months after 1 Thessalonians.
audience: The church in Thessalonica.
setting: Persecution had intensified. Some had been shaken by a false claim, perhaps in a letter claiming to be from Paul, that the day of the Lord had already come. Others had stopped working.
purpose: To encourage the persecuted church with God's justice, correct confusion about the day of the Lord, and command the idle to work.
structure: Encouragement in persecution (1), events before the day of the Lord (2), and instructions on prayer and idleness (3).

## themes
- Endurance | Paul boasts of their steadfastness and faith in persecution.
- God's Justice | God will repay those who afflict His people when Jesus is revealed.
- The Day of the Lord | It will not come before the rebellion and the man of lawlessness are revealed.
- God's Choice and Calling | God chose them to be saved through sanctification and belief in truth.
- Work | "If anyone is not willing to work, let him not eat" (3:10).
- Faithfulness of God | "The Lord is faithful. He will establish you and guard you" (3:3).

@ Glorified in His Saints
Paul thanks God that their faith is growing and their love increasing, and he boasts of their steadfastness in persecution. God will repay affliction to those who afflict them and grant relief when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with His angels. Paul prays that God will make them worthy of His calling and that Jesus' name will be glorified in them.

@ The Man of Lawlessness
Paul tells them not to be shaken by claims that the day of the Lord has come. First the rebellion must come and the man of lawlessness be revealed, who exalts himself and takes his seat in God's temple. Something now restrains him. The Lord Jesus will destroy him by His appearing. Those who refuse to love the truth will be deceived. But God chose believers for salvation, so stand firm.

@ Do Not Grow Weary
Paul asks for prayer that the word would speed ahead. The Lord is faithful and will guard them. Paul commands them to keep away from idle believers. He worked night and day to set an example: "If anyone is not willing to work, let him not eat." Do not grow weary in doing good. Warn the disobedient as brothers. "The Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times in every way."
