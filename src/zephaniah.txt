tagline: The great day of the LORD, and the God who sings over His people
author: Zephaniah son of Cushi, a descendant of King Hezekiah (1:1).
date: During the reign of Josiah (640-609 BC), likely before Josiah's reforms of 622 BC.
audience: Judah and Jerusalem, along with surrounding nations.
setting: After the wicked reigns of Manasseh and Amon, Judah was filled with Baal worship, astrology, and complacency.
purpose: To warn of the coming day of the LORD on Judah and the nations, call the humble to seek God, and promise a purified remnant in whom God delights.
structure: The day of the LORD against Judah (1), a call to repentance and judgment on the nations (2), and judgment on Jerusalem followed by restoration and joy (3).

## themes
- The Day of the LORD | A great and bitter day of wrath on sin, near and hastening fast.
- Complacency | God punishes those who say, "The LORD will not do good, nor will he do ill" (1:12).
- Humility | "Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land" (2:3).
- Universal Judgment | Nations around Judah are judged as well.
- The Remnant | God leaves a humble and lowly people who seek refuge in Him.
- God's Joy | "He will rejoice over you with gladness... he will exult over you with loud singing" (3:17).

## christ
- 3:15 | "The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst" is fulfilled in Jesus, King of Israel (John 1:49; 12:13).
- 3:9 | God giving the peoples pure speech to call on His name anticipates Pentecost (Acts 2).
- 3:17 | The mighty one who saves and rejoices over His people is revealed in Christ (Luke 15:7).

@ Seek the LORD, You Humble
Zephaniah calls the shameless nation to gather before the decree. "Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land... seek righteousness; seek humility; perhaps you may be hidden on the day of the anger of the LORD." Judgment falls on Philistia, Moab, Ammon, Cush, and Assyria. Nineveh will become desolate.

@ The LORD Rejoices Over You
Jerusalem is a rebellious, oppressive city whose leaders are corrupt. But God will change the speech of peoples so they call on His name, and leave a humble remnant. "Sing aloud, O daughter of Zion!... The LORD your God is in your midst, a mighty one who will save; he will rejoice over you with gladness... he will exult over you with loud singing." He will gather the outcasts and change their shame into praise.
