tagline: Solomon's glory, a divided kingdom, and Elijah's stand for God
author: Not stated. Jewish tradition credits Jeremiah. The author used court records such as "the Book of the Acts of Solomon" (11:41). 1 and 2 Kings were originally one book.
date: The events cover about 971-853 BC. The completed Kings was written during the exile, after 561 BC (2 Kings 25:27).
audience: The exiles in Babylon, who needed to understand why the kingdom fell and that God was still faithful to His covenant with David.
setting: Jerusalem and the united kingdom under Solomon, then the divided kingdoms of Israel (north, capital Samaria) and Judah (south, capital Jerusalem).
purpose: To explain Israel's decline as the result of idolatry and covenant unfaithfulness, measuring each king by his loyalty to the LORD, while showing God's patience through His prophets.
structure: Solomon's reign (1-11), the divided kingdom (12-16), and Elijah's ministry during Ahab's reign (17-22).

## themes
- Faithfulness Brings Blessing | Kings are judged by whether they walk with God "as David his father" did.
- The Temple and God's Presence | God's name dwells in the temple, yet "heaven cannot contain" Him (8:27).
- Wisdom | Solomon asks for wisdom and receives it, but later fails to live by it.
- Idolatry Divides | Solomon's foreign wives turn his heart, leading to the kingdom's division.
- The Prophetic Word | God's word through prophets like Ahijah, Elijah, and Micaiah always comes true.
- The LORD Versus Baal | At Mount Carmel, Elijah shows that the LORD alone is God.

## reflect
- Solomon asked for wisdom rather than riches. What would you ask God for, and why?
- Solomon's heart turned slowly through compromises. What relationships or influences could be turning your heart?
- Elijah asked, "How long will you limp between two opinions?" Where are you trying to serve two masters?
- After his great victory, Elijah fell into despair. How does God's gentle care for him (rest, food, His presence) speak to you?
- God spoke in a low whisper. How can you make space to hear Him?
