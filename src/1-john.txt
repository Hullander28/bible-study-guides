tagline: That you may know you have eternal life: walking in light, love, and truth
author: John the apostle, an eyewitness of Jesus (1:1-3), according to early church tradition.
date: About AD 85-95, probably from Ephesus.
audience: Churches in Asia Minor that John cared for as "little children."
setting: False teachers had left the church (2:19), denying that Jesus came in the flesh and claiming that sin did not matter, which unsettled believers.
purpose: To give believers assurance of eternal life and to show the marks of true faith: right belief about Jesus, obedience to God's commands, and love for one another.
structure: A spiraling meditation that returns repeatedly to three tests (truth, obedience, and love), arranged around the themes of God as light (1-2), God's children (3), God as love (4), and assurance through faith (5).

## chapters
@ Walking in the Light
John proclaims the Word of life, which he heard, saw, and touched, so that his readers may have fellowship with the Father and the Son. God is light, and in Him is no darkness. If we walk in the light, the blood of Jesus cleanses us. If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, but if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive.

@ Our Advocate and the Danger of the World
If anyone sins, we have an advocate, Jesus Christ the righteous, the propitiation for our sins. Whoever says he knows God must keep His commands and walk as Jesus walked. Whoever hates his brother is in darkness. Do not love the world: the desires of the flesh, the desires of the eyes, and the pride of life. Antichrists have gone out from us. Abide in Him.

@ Children of God
"See what kind of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God." When He appears we shall be like Him. Everyone who is born of God does not keep on sinning. The Son came to destroy the devil's works. We know love because Jesus laid down His life for us; we ought to love "in deed and in truth." When our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart.

@ God Is Love
Test the spirits: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God. "He who is in you is greater than he who is in the world." Love one another, for love is from God; God is love. God showed His love by sending His Son as a propitiation. Perfect love casts out fear. "We love because he first loved us." Whoever loves God must love his brother.

@ Faith That Overcomes
Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ is born of God, and faith overcomes the world. God has testified about His Son. "Whoever has the Son has life." John writes so that believers may know they have eternal life. God hears us when we ask according to His will. Keep yourselves from idols.
