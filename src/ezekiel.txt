tagline: The glory departs and returns: "Then they will know that I am the LORD"
author: Ezekiel son of Buzi, a priest taken to Babylon in 597 BC with King Jehoiachin (1:1-3).
date: Ezekiel's visions are dated from 593 BC to 571 BC.
audience: Jewish exiles in Babylon by the Chebar canal, who doubted God's justice and clung to hope that Jerusalem could not fall.
setting: Babylon. The first half is before Jerusalem's fall (586 BC), and the second half comes after news of its destruction reaches the exiles (33:21).
purpose: To show that Jerusalem's fall was God's just judgment, call individuals to repentance, and promise a new heart, a new David, a renewed people, and God's glory dwelling with them again.
structure: Ezekiel's call (1-3), judgment on Judah and Jerusalem (4-24), oracles against the nations (25-32), and restoration (33-48), ending with the vision of a new temple.

## themes
- The Glory of God | God's glory appears to Ezekiel, departs from the temple, and returns to the new temple.
- "Then They Will Know That I Am the LORD" | This refrain, used over 60 times, shows God's purpose to make Himself known.
- God's Holiness | Israel's idolatry defiled God's name, and God acts for His holy name's sake.
- Personal Responsibility | "The soul who sins shall die" (18:4), and God takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked.
- The Watchman | Ezekiel must warn the wicked; silence makes him accountable.
- New Heart and New Spirit | God promises to remove the heart of stone and put His Spirit within (36:26-27).
- The Good Shepherd | God Himself will shepherd His flock through His servant David (34).
- Resurrection and Renewal | Dry bones live by the Spirit, and a river flows from the temple bringing life.

## christ
- 34:11-24 | God as the shepherd who seeks the lost is fulfilled in Jesus, the Good Shepherd (John 10:11; Luke 19:10).
- 36:25-27 | The promise of cleansing water and the Spirit underlies Jesus' words to Nicodemus (John 3:5).
- 37:24-25 | "David my servant" as eternal king points to Jesus, the Son of David.
- 1:26-28 | The likeness of a man on the throne foreshadows the glorified Son (Revelation 1:13-17).
- 47:1-12 | The river of life flowing from the temple echoes Jesus' living water (John 7:37-39; Revelation 22:1-2).
