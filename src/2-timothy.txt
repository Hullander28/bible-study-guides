tagline: Guard the gospel, endure hardship, and finish the race
author: The apostle Paul (1:1), writing his final letter.
date: About AD 64-67, from a Roman prison shortly before his execution under Nero.
audience: Timothy, Paul's beloved son in the faith, still ministering in Ephesus.
setting: Paul is in chains, deserted by many, expecting death. False teaching and persecution threatened the churches.
purpose: To urge Timothy to be unashamed of the gospel, suffer as a good soldier, guard and preach the word, and come to Paul quickly.
structure: Be unashamed and guard the gospel (1), endure as a soldier, athlete, and farmer (2), know Scripture amid godless times (3), and preach the word as Paul finishes his race (4).

## chapters
@ Not Ashamed of the Gospel
Paul thanks God for Timothy's sincere faith, which first lived in his grandmother Lois and mother Eunice. "Fan into flame the gift of God." God gave a spirit of power, love, and self-control, not fear. Do not be ashamed; share in suffering. Christ abolished death. Paul is not ashamed, for he knows whom he has believed. Guard the good deposit. Onesiphorus was not ashamed of Paul's chains.

@ A Good Soldier of Christ
Be strengthened by grace and entrust the gospel to faithful people who will teach others. Share in suffering like a soldier, compete by the rules like an athlete, work hard like a farmer. Remember Jesus Christ, risen. If we are faithless, He remains faithful. Rightly handle the word of truth. Flee youthful passions; pursue righteousness. The Lord's servant must be kind and patiently correct opponents.

@ The Sufficiency of Scripture
In the last days people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, proud, and lovers of pleasure rather than God, having a form of godliness but denying its power. Timothy has followed Paul's teaching and sufferings. All who desire to live godly lives will be persecuted. "All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness."
? Which traits of the last days do you see in our culture (3:1-5)?
? Why should godly people expect persecution (3:12)?
? What does 3:16-17 teach about the Bible?

@ Finish the Race
Paul solemnly charges Timothy: "Preach the word; be ready in season and out of season." People will gather teachers to suit their passions. Do the work of an evangelist. Paul is being poured out as a drink offering: "I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith." Many deserted him, but the Lord stood by him. He asks Timothy to come quickly and bring Mark.

? Why do people seek teachers who tell them what they want (4:3-4)?
? How does Paul face death with confidence (4:6-8)?
