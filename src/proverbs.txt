tagline: Practical wisdom for everyday life, rooted in the fear of the LORD
author: Mainly Solomon (1:1; 10:1; 25:1), with sections by "the wise" (22:17; 24:23), Agur (30), and King Lemuel (31). Hezekiah's men copied some of Solomon's proverbs (25:1).
date: Most proverbs come from Solomon's reign (about 970-930 BC). The collection was completed around Hezekiah's time (about 700 BC) or later.
audience: Originally young men being trained for life and leadership ("my son"), and all of God's people seeking skill in godly living.
setting: Israel's royal court and households, applied to everyday life: family, work, speech, money, friendship, and justice.
purpose: To teach wisdom, the skill of living well in God's world, grounded in the fear of the LORD, and to contrast the path of wisdom with the path of folly.
structure: Long poems on wisdom (1-9), short proverbs of Solomon (10:1-22:16), sayings of the wise (22:17-24:34), Hezekiah's collection (25-29), and the words of Agur and Lemuel, including the excellent wife (30-31).

## themes
- The Fear of the LORD | Reverent trust in God is the beginning of wisdom and knowledge (1:7; 9:10).
- Two Paths | Wisdom leads to life and folly to death. Proverbs constantly contrasts the righteous and the wicked, the wise and the fool.
- The Power of Words | The tongue can bring life or death, healing or harm (18:21).
- Work and Diligence | The diligent prosper, while the sluggard comes to poverty.
- Money and Generosity | Wealth is fleeting; honesty, generosity, and contentment are better.
- Family and Friendship | Parents teach, children listen, and true friends love at all times.
- Sexual Purity | Repeated warnings against the adulterous woman and a call to delight in one's spouse.
## verses
- Proverbs 1:7 | The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge; fools despise wisdom and instruction.
- Proverbs 3:5-6 | Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths.
- Proverbs 4:23 | Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life.
- Proverbs 16:9 | The heart of man plans his way, but the LORD establishes his steps.
## christ
- 8:22-31 | Wisdom present with God at creation anticipates Christ, through whom all things were made (John 1:1-3; Colossians 1:15-17).
- 1 Corinthians 1:24, 30 | Christ is "the wisdom of God" who "became to us wisdom from God."
- 30:4 | Agur asks, "What is his name, and what is his son's name?" pointing to the Son revealed in Jesus.
- Colossians 2:3 | In Christ "are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge."
