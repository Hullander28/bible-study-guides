tagline: Every spiritual blessing in Christ, and a life worthy of the calling
author: The apostle Paul (1:1; 3:1), writing as a prisoner.
date: About AD 60-62, during Paul's first Roman imprisonment.
audience: The saints in Ephesus and likely other churches in Asia Minor (some early manuscripts lack "in Ephesus").
setting: Paul had spent three years in Ephesus (Acts 19-20). The church included Jews and Gentiles in a city devoted to Artemis and the occult.
purpose: To reveal God's eternal plan to unite all things in Christ, describe the believer's riches in Him, and show how the church lives out its new identity in unity, holiness, family life, and spiritual warfare.
structure: Doctrine, who we are in Christ (1-3), and practice, how we walk in Christ (4-6).

## themes
- In Christ | Paul repeatedly emphasizes what believers have "in Christ."
- God's Eternal Plan | God chose us before the foundation of the world to unite all things in Christ.
- Grace | "By grace you have been saved through faith" (2:8).
- The Church | Christ's body, God's temple, and His bride, uniting Jew and Gentile.
- Unity and Maturity | Gifts are given to build up the body in love.
- The New Life | Put off the old self and put on the new.
- Spiritual Warfare | Stand firm in the armor of God.

## chapters
@ Every Spiritual Blessing
Paul praises God, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing: chosen before creation, adopted, redeemed by His blood, forgiven, made heirs, and sealed with the Holy Spirit. He prays that believers would know the hope of their calling, the riches of their inheritance, and the greatness of God's power shown in raising Christ.

@ Saved by Grace, Made One
We were dead in trespasses and sins, "But God, being rich in mercy," made us alive with Christ. "By grace you have been saved through faith... not a result of works." We are His workmanship. Christ has broken down the wall between Jew and Gentile, making one new man, reconciled through the cross and built into a holy temple.

@ The Mystery Revealed
Paul explains the mystery: Gentiles are fellow heirs and members of the same body through the gospel. He was made a minister of this grace. He prays that believers would be strengthened by the Spirit, that Christ would dwell in their hearts, and that they would grasp the breadth, length, height, and depth of Christ's love.

@ Unity and the New Self
Walk worthy of your calling with humility, gentleness, and patience, maintaining unity: one body, one Spirit, one Lord, one faith. Christ gave leaders to equip the saints for ministry so the body grows to maturity, speaking the truth in love. Put off the old self, be renewed, and put on the new self. Be kind and forgiving.

@ Walk in Love, Light, and Wisdom
Be imitators of God and walk in love, as Christ loved us. Avoid sexual immorality and impurity. Walk as children of light. Make the best use of time. Be filled with the Spirit, singing and giving thanks. Submit to one another. Wives submit to husbands as to the Lord; husbands love wives as Christ loved the church.

@ The Armor of God
Children obey parents; fathers do not provoke children. Servants work as to the Lord; masters treat servants fairly. Be strong in the Lord and put on the whole armor of God: the belt of truth, breastplate of righteousness, gospel shoes, shield of faith, helmet of salvation, and sword of the Spirit, the word of God, praying at all times.
