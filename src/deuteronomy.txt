tagline: Moses' farewell sermons: remember, love, and obey the LORD
author: Moses, who delivers these speeches and writes "this law" (31:9, 24). The account of his death (ch. 34) was likely added by Joshua or a later inspired writer.
date: About 1406 BC on the traditional date, in the final weeks of Moses' life, on the plains of Moab.
audience: The new generation of Israel about to cross the Jordan into Canaan without Moses.
setting: The plains of Moab, east of the Jordan, opposite Jericho, at the end of forty years in the wilderness.
purpose: To renew the covenant with the new generation, calling them to remember God's faithfulness, love Him with all their heart, obey His law in the land, and choose life.
structure: Three speeches of Moses: a review of history (1-4), the covenant law expounded (5-26), and covenant renewal with blessings and curses (27-30), followed by Moses' last words and death (31-34).

## themes
- Love for God | "You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart" (6:5). Obedience flows from wholehearted love.
- Remember and Do Not Forget | The word "remember" recurs often. Prosperity can tempt Israel to forget the God who saved them (8:11-18).
- Covenant Loyalty | Blessing comes through faithfulness to the covenant, and curse through rebellion (28; 30:15-20).
- Grace and Election | God chose Israel not because they were great or righteous, but because He loved them (7:7-8; 9:4-6).
- One God, One Place of Worship | The LORD is one, and Israel must worship Him alone, where He chooses, and destroy idols (6:4; 12).
- Justice and Compassion | Laws protect the poor, widows, orphans, sojourners, and even animals, reflecting God's heart (10:17-19).
- Teaching the Next Generation | Parents must teach God's words diligently to their children (6:6-9).

## reflect
- Deuteronomy tells Israel to remember God's past faithfulness again and again. Write down three ways God has been faithful to you.
- How are you teaching God's words to the next generation in your home, at meals, and on the road (6:7)?
- Moses warns that comfort can make us forget God (8:11-18). Where has success or ease dulled your devotion?
- God chose Israel out of love, not merit (7:7-8). How does that free you from trying to earn His love?
- What does it mean for you to "choose life" (30:19) in a decision you are facing?
- Moses finished his life well though he could not enter the land. How do you want to finish your race?
