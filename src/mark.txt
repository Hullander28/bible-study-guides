tagline: Jesus the Servant Son of God who came to give His life
author: John Mark, companion of Paul and Barnabas and later of Peter, whose preaching early tradition says Mark recorded (Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13).
date: Probably the mid-50s to late 60s AD, likely from Rome.
audience: Primarily Gentile Christians, likely in Rome, facing persecution. Mark explains Jewish customs and translates Aramaic words.
setting: Jesus' ministry in Galilee and His final week in Jerusalem.
purpose: To present Jesus as the Christ, the Son of God, who acts with authority and came to serve and suffer, and to call disciples to follow Him on the way of the cross.
structure: A fast-moving narrative ("immediately" appears often). Jesus' Galilean ministry (1-8:26), the turning point at Peter's confession and the journey to Jerusalem (8:27-10:52), and the passion week and resurrection (11-16).

## themes
- Jesus the Son of God | Mark begins and climaxes (15:39) with this confession.
- Authority | Jesus has authority over demons, disease, nature, sin, and the Sabbath.
- The Suffering Servant | "The Son of Man came not to be served but to serve" (10:45).
- The Messianic Secret | Jesus often tells people not to reveal Him until the cross reveals who He is.
- Discipleship and Failure | The disciples misunderstand repeatedly, yet Jesus keeps calling them.
- Faith and Fear | Jesus calls people from fear to faith.
- Urgency | The kingdom has arrived, and Mark's narrative moves "immediately."

## chapters
@ The Beginning of the Gospel
"The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God." John baptizes; Jesus is baptized and hears the Father's voice. He is tempted in the wilderness, preaches the kingdom, calls fishermen, casts out an unclean spirit, heals Peter's mother-in-law and many others, prays in a desolate place, and cleanses a leper.
? What does Mark's first verse tell you about his purpose?
? What does it mean to "repent and believe in the gospel" (1:15)?
? What does Jesus' early morning prayer (1:35) teach?

Four friends lower a paralytic through a roof; Jesus forgives and heals him. He calls Levi and eats with sinners: "I came not to call the righteous, but sinners." He teaches about new wine in new wineskins and declares that the Son of Man is lord of the Sabbath.

? Why did forgiving sins offend the scribes?
? How does Jesus see the Sabbath (2:27-28)?

@ The Twelve and Jesus' True Family
Jesus heals a withered hand on the Sabbath, and the Pharisees plot with the Herodians. He appoints the twelve "so that they might be with him" and be sent out. His family thinks He is out of His mind, and scribes accuse Him of Beelzebul. "Whoever does the will of God, he is my brother and sister and mother."

? What is Jesus' answer to the Beelzebul charge?
? Who belongs to Jesus' family (3:35)?

@ Parables and the Storm
Jesus teaches the parable of the sower, the lamp, the growing seed, and the mustard seed. That evening He calms a great storm: "Why are you so afraid? Have you still no faith?" The disciples ask, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

@ Power Over Demons, Disease, and Death
Jesus frees a man possessed by a legion of demons, who then tells what Jesus did for him. A woman with a 12-year bleeding touches His garment and is healed: "Your faith has made you well." Jesus raises Jairus's daughter: "Talitha cumi."
? How did the healed man respond, and how did the townspeople?
? Why did Jesus stop to find the woman who touched Him?
? How does "Do not fear, only believe" (5:36) speak to you?

@ Rejected and Sent
Jesus is rejected in Nazareth. He sends out the twelve. Herod beheads John. Jesus has compassion on the crowd and feeds five thousand. He walks on the sea, telling the frightened disciples, "Take heart; it is I. Do not be afraid." He heals many in Gennesaret.
? Why did familiarity breed unbelief in Nazareth?

The Pharisees criticize unwashed hands. Jesus says they abandon God's commandment for tradition, and that what comes out of the heart defiles. A Syrophoenician woman's faith wins her daughter's healing. Jesus heals a deaf man: "Ephphatha," "Be opened."

? What evils come from the heart (7:21-23)?

> Isaiah 29:13 | Matthew 15 | Isaiah 35:5-6
@ Who Do You Say I Am?
Jesus feeds four thousand. The disciples still do not understand. Jesus heals a blind man in two stages. Peter confesses, "You are the Christ." Jesus teaches that the Son of Man must suffer, die, and rise, rebukes Peter, and calls disciples to take up their cross.
? What does the two-stage healing picture about the disciples' understanding?
? Why did Peter reject Jesus' prediction?
? What does it profit a man to gain the whole world and lose his soul?

@ Transfiguration and Greatness
Jesus is transfigured before Peter, James, and John. He heals a boy whose father cries, "I believe; help my unbelief!" He again predicts His death. The disciples argue about who is greatest; Jesus says, "If anyone would be first, he must be last of all and servant of all."
? How does the transfiguration prepare the disciples for the cross?
? What does the father's prayer (9:24) teach about faith?
? How does Jesus redefine greatness (9:35-37)?

Jesus teaches on marriage, blesses children, and loves a rich man who goes away sad. He predicts His death a third time. James and John ask for glory; Jesus says He came "to give his life as a ransom for many." Blind Bartimaeus cries for mercy, is healed, and follows Jesus on the way.

> Daniel 7:13 | Psalm 110:1 | Hebrews 5:7-8
@ The Crucifixion
Pilate releases Barabbas and hands Jesus over to be crucified. Soldiers mock Him as king. Simon of Cyrene carries His cross. Darkness falls; Jesus cries, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" The temple curtain tears, and the centurion says, "Truly this man was the Son of God!" Joseph of Arimathea buries Him.
