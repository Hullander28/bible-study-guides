tagline: Faith that works: practical wisdom for genuine believers
author: James, the brother of Jesus and leader of the Jerusalem church (1:1; Galatians 1:19; Acts 15:13).
date: Probably about AD 45-49, possibly the earliest New Testament book. James was martyred about AD 62.
audience: "The twelve tribes in the Dispersion," Jewish Christians scattered outside Palestine.
setting: These believers faced trials, poverty, and oppression by the rich, along with favoritism, harmful speech, and quarrels in their communities.
purpose: To show that genuine faith produces a changed life, enduring trials, doing the word, caring for the poor, controlling the tongue, and humbly submitting to God.
structure: A collection of practical teachings, similar to Proverbs and the Sermon on the Mount, on trials, faith and works, the tongue, wisdom, humility, wealth, patience, and prayer.

## themes
- Trials | Testing produces steadfastness and maturity.
- Wisdom | Ask God for wisdom; true wisdom is pure, peaceable, and gentle.
- Hearing and Doing | Be doers of the word, not hearers only.
- Faith and Works | Faith without works is dead.
- The Tongue | Small but powerful for blessing or destruction.
- Humility | God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.
- Rich and Poor | God chose the poor to be rich in faith; the oppressive rich will be judged.
- Prayer | The prayer of a righteous person has great power.

## chapters
@ Trials and Doing the Word
"Count it all joy... when you meet trials," for testing produces steadfastness. Ask God for wisdom in faith. The lowly should boast in exaltation, the rich in humiliation. God tempts no one; every good gift comes from the Father of lights. Be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger. Be doers of the word. Pure religion is caring for orphans and widows and keeping oneself unstained.

@ Faith That Works
Do not show partiality by favoring the rich over the poor. God chose the poor to be rich in faith. Fulfill the royal law: love your neighbor as yourself. What good is faith without works? Even demons believe and shudder. Abraham and Rahab showed their faith by their actions. "Faith apart from works is dead."

@ Taming the Tongue
Not many should become teachers. The tongue is small, like a bit or a rudder, but it is a fire. No human can tame it; with it we bless God and curse people made in His image. Earthly wisdom shows itself in jealousy and selfish ambition, but wisdom from above is pure, peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits.

@ Submit to God
Quarrels come from passions at war within. You do not have because you do not ask, or ask wrongly. Friendship with the world is enmity with God. "God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble." Submit to God, resist the devil, draw near to God. Do not speak evil against one another. Do not boast about tomorrow; say, "If the Lord wills."

@ Patience and Prayer
The rich who hoard and defraud workers will face judgment. Be patient until the coming of the Lord, like a farmer waiting for rain. Do not grumble against one another. Remember the steadfastness of Job. Let your yes be yes. Pray in suffering, sing in joy, call the elders to pray for the sick, confess sins to one another. Elijah prayed and God answered. Bring back a wanderer from the truth.
