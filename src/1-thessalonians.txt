tagline: Faith, love, and hope while waiting for the Lord's return
author: The apostle Paul, with Silvanus (Silas) and Timothy (1:1).
date: About AD 50-51, from Corinth, making it one of Paul's earliest letters.
audience: The young church in Thessalonica, a major city in Macedonia, mostly Gentile converts who had turned from idols.
setting: Paul had been forced to leave Thessalonica after a few weeks because of persecution (Acts 17:1-10). Timothy brought back a good report, along with questions about believers who had died.
purpose: To encourage a persecuted young church, defend Paul's sincere ministry, urge holy living, and comfort them with the hope of Christ's return and the resurrection.
structure: Thanksgiving and Paul's ministry among them (1-3), and instructions on holiness, love, the Lord's coming, and church life (4-5).

## chapters
@ A Model Church
Paul thanks God for the Thessalonians' work of faith, labor of love, and steadfastness of hope. The gospel came to them not only in word but in power and the Holy Spirit. They received it in much affliction with joy and became an example to believers everywhere, turning from idols to serve God and wait for His Son from heaven.
? What do faith, love, and hope look like in action (1:3)?
? How did the gospel come to them (1:5)?
? How does 1:9-10 summarize conversion?
> Acts 17:1-9 | 1 Corinthians 13:13 | Titus 2:13
@ Paul's Ministry Among Them
Paul reminds them that his ministry was not from deceit or flattery or greed. He was gentle like a nursing mother and cared like a father, sharing not only the gospel but his own life. They received God's word as truly God's word. Paul longs to see them; they are his glory and joy.
? What motives did Paul reject in ministry (2:3-6)?
? How do the mother and father images describe good spiritual care?
? What does it mean to receive Scripture as God's word (2:13)?
> 2 Corinthians 4:2 | Acts 20:33-35 | Hebrews 4:12
@ Timothy's Good Report
Unable to bear it, Paul sent Timothy to strengthen them in afflictions, which believers are destined for. Timothy returns with good news of their faith and love, and Paul is comforted: "Now we live, if you are standing fast in the Lord." He prays that their love would increase and their hearts be established in holiness.
? Why did Paul send Timothy (3:2-5)?
? Why does Paul say suffering should not surprise believers (3:3-4)?
? Pray Paul's prayer in 3:12-13 for someone you love.
> Acts 14:22 | 2 Timothy 3:12 | Philippians 1:9-11
@ Holiness and the Coming of the Lord
Paul urges them to please God more and more. God's will is their sanctification: abstaining from sexual immorality and controlling their bodies in holiness. They should love one another, live quietly, and work with their hands. Believers who have died will not miss out: the Lord will descend, the dead in Christ will rise first, and "we will always be with the Lord. Therefore encourage one another."
? What does God's will for your sanctification involve (4:3-8)?
? Why is a quiet, hardworking life a witness (4:11-12)?
? How does 4:13-18 give hope in grief?
> 1 Corinthians 15:51-55 | John 14:1-3 | 1 Corinthians 6:18-20
@ Children of the Day
The day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night, but believers are children of light. So stay awake and be sober, wearing faith, love, and the hope of salvation. God has destined us for salvation, not wrath. Respect leaders, admonish the idle, encourage the fainthearted, help the weak. "Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in all circumstances." God will sanctify you completely.
