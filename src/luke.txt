tagline: The Savior who came to seek and to save the lost
author: Luke, a physician and companion of Paul (Colossians 4:14; 2 Timothy 4:11), who also wrote Acts.
date: About AD 60-62, likely before Acts was completed.
audience: Theophilus (1:3) and a broader Gentile audience.
setting: Jesus' birth, ministry in Galilee, long journey to Jerusalem, death, and resurrection, set carefully in world history.
purpose: To give an orderly, carefully researched account so readers may have certainty about Jesus, the Savior for all people, especially the poor, outcasts, women, and sinners.
structure: Prologue (1:1-4), births of John and Jesus (1-2), preparation (3-4:13), Galilean ministry (4:14-9:50), the journey to Jerusalem (9:51-19:27), and ministry, death, and resurrection in Jerusalem (19:28-24:53).

## themes
- Salvation for All | Jesus brings good news to Gentiles, Samaritans, the poor, women, and sinners.
- Seeking the Lost | "The Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost" (19:10).
- The Holy Spirit | The Spirit is active in births, Jesus' ministry, and the promise of power.
- Prayer | Luke shows Jesus praying at every key moment.
- Joy and Praise | Songs and rejoicing fill the Gospel.
- Wealth and Poverty | Jesus warns the rich and lifts the lowly.
- Fulfilled Scripture | Everything written about Jesus must be fulfilled (24:44).

## chapters
@ Two Births Foretold
Luke writes an orderly account for Theophilus. Gabriel tells Zechariah that Elizabeth will bear John, who will prepare the way. Gabriel tells Mary she will conceive the Son of the Most High by the Holy Spirit. Mary says, "Let it be to me according to your word." She visits Elizabeth and sings the Magnificat. John is born, and Zechariah prophesies.
? Why does Luke emphasize careful research (1:1-4)?
? How do Zechariah's and Mary's responses to Gabriel differ?
? What does the Magnificat reveal about God (1:46-55)?
> 1 Samuel 2:1-10 | Isaiah 7:14 | Malachi 4:5-6
@ Jesus' Birth and Childhood
Jesus is born in Bethlehem and laid in a manger. Angels announce good news of great joy to shepherds. Simeon and Anna recognize Jesus as the Lord's salvation in the temple. At twelve, Jesus stays in the temple: "Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house?" He increases in wisdom and stature.
? Why were shepherds the first to hear?
? What does Simeon prophesy about Jesus (2:29-35)?
? What does 2:49 reveal about Jesus' self-understanding?
> Micah 5:2 | Isaiah 9:6 | Isaiah 49:6
@ John's Ministry and Jesus' Baptism
John preaches repentance and gives practical instructions to crowds, tax collectors, and soldiers. Jesus is baptized while praying, and the Spirit descends. Luke traces Jesus' genealogy back to "Adam, the son of God."
? What practical fruits of repentance did John call for (3:10-14)?
? Why does Luke trace Jesus back to Adam?
? What is significant about Jesus praying at His baptism?
> Isaiah 40:3-5 | Matthew 3 | Romans 5:12-19
@ Temptation and the Nazareth Sermon
Jesus resists the devil's temptations with Scripture. In Nazareth He reads Isaiah 61: "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me... to proclaim good news to the poor." He says, "Today this Scripture has been fulfilled." The townspeople try to throw Him off a cliff. He heals and casts out demons in Capernaum.
? What is Jesus' mission according to 4:18-19?
? Why did the hometown crowd turn against Him?
? How does Jesus balance ministry and solitude (4:42)?
> Isaiah 61:1-2 | Deuteronomy 6:13, 16 | 1 Kings 17:8-24
@ Fishers of Men
After a miraculous catch, Peter falls down: "Depart from me, for I am a sinful man." Jesus says, "From now on you will be catching men." He heals a leper and a paralytic, calls Levi, and says He came to call sinners to repentance.
? Why did the catch of fish make Peter feel sinful?
? How did Jesus often respond to growing crowds (5:16)?
? What does new wine in new wineskins mean?
> Isaiah 6:5 | Mark 2:1-22 | Matthew 9:1-17
@ The Sermon on the Plain
Jesus prays all night and chooses the twelve. He teaches blessings and woes, love for enemies, "Be merciful, even as your Father is merciful," not judging, good trees bearing good fruit, and building on the rock.
? Why did Jesus pray all night before choosing the twelve?
? How do the blessings and woes reverse worldly values?
? Who is an "enemy" you need to love and bless?
> Matthew 5-7 | Romans 12:14-21 | James 1:22
@ Faith and Forgiveness
Jesus marvels at a centurion's faith and raises a widow's son in Nain. He answers John's question. A sinful woman weeps on Jesus' feet and anoints Him. Jesus tells Simon the Pharisee that she loved much because she was forgiven much.
? What moved Jesus to raise the widow's son (7:13)?
? How did Simon and the woman see Jesus differently?
? How does being forgiven much produce great love (7:47)?
> 1 Kings 17:17-24 | Isaiah 35:5-6 | 1 John 4:19
@ The Sower and Power Over All
Women support Jesus' ministry. He tells the parable of the sower. His true family hears and does God's word. He calms a storm, frees the Gerasene demoniac, heals a woman who touches Him, and raises Jairus's daughter.
? What role did women play in Jesus' ministry (8:1-3)?
? What does it mean to hold the word "in an honest and good heart" (8:15)?
? What does each miracle in this chapter reveal about Jesus?
> Mark 4-5 | Matthew 13 | James 1:21
@ The Christ of God
Jesus sends out the twelve, feeds five thousand, and Peter confesses Him as "the Christ of God." Jesus calls disciples to take up their cross daily. He is transfigured, discussing His "departure" (exodus) with Moses and Elijah. He sets His face to go to Jerusalem. Would-be followers are told to count the cost.
? Why does Luke say "take up his cross daily" (9:23)?
? What is the "departure" or exodus Jesus would accomplish (9:31)?
? What excuses keep people from following Jesus (9:57-62)?
> Mark 8-9 | Matthew 16-17 | Isaiah 50:7
@ The Good Samaritan
Jesus sends out seventy-two, who return rejoicing. He rejoices in the Spirit. A lawyer asks, "Who is my neighbor?" Jesus tells of a Samaritan who shows mercy to a beaten man. At Martha's home, Mary sits at His feet: "Mary has chosen the good portion."
? Why should we rejoice more that our names are in heaven (10:20)?
? Who is the "neighbor" you are called to love?
? Are you more like Martha or Mary right now?

? What does the friend at midnight teach about persistence?
? How does Jesus warn against religious hypocrisy?

@ Do Not Be Anxious
Jesus warns against hypocrisy and the fear of man. He tells the parable of the rich fool: "One's life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions." Do not be anxious; seek His kingdom. Be ready like servants waiting for their master. He brings division, not superficial peace.
? What was the rich fool's mistake?
? How does the Father's care free you from anxiety (12:22-32)?
? What does "to whom much was given, of him much will be required" mean for you (12:48)?
> Matthew 6:19-34 | 1 Timothy 6:17-19 | Ecclesiastes 2:18-21

Jesus says that unless people repent, they will perish. He tells of a fruitless fig tree given one more year. He heals a woman bent over for 18 years on the Sabbath. The kingdom is like mustard seed and leaven. Strive to enter the narrow door. He laments over Jerusalem.
? What does the fig tree parable say about God's patience?
? Why were leaders angry about healing on the Sabbath?
? What does striving to enter the narrow door involve (13:24)?
> 2 Peter 3:9 | Matthew 7:13-14 | Matthew 23:37-39
@ The Great Banquet
At a Pharisee's dinner, Jesus heals on the Sabbath and teaches humility: take the lowest seat. Invite the poor and disabled. In the parable of the great banquet, invited guests make excuses, so the master invites the poor and outcasts. Jesus says disciples must count the cost.
? What does Jesus teach about humility at the table?
? Who is invited to God's banquet?
? Why must disciples count the cost (14:28-33)?
> Proverbs 25:6-7 | Matthew 22:1-14 | Philippians 3:7-8
@ Lost and Found
Tax collectors and sinners draw near, and Pharisees grumble. Jesus tells three parables: a lost sheep, a lost coin, and a lost son. The father runs to embrace his returning son and throws a feast. The older brother refuses to celebrate.
? What do the three parables have in common?
? What does the father's running and embrace show about God?
? How might you be like the older brother?
> Ezekiel 34:11-16 | Isaiah 55:7 | 1 Timothy 1:15
@ Money and Eternity
Jesus tells of a shrewd manager and teaches faithfulness with money: "You cannot serve God and money." The Pharisees, lovers of money, scoff. In the story of the rich man and Lazarus, the rich man is tormented while Lazarus is comforted, and Abraham says even resurrection won't persuade those who ignore Moses and the Prophets.
? How can you use money for eternal purposes (16:9)?
? What does the rich man and Lazarus teach about eternity?

Jesus walks with two disciples to Emmaus, explaining all the Scriptures about Himself, and is known in the breaking of bread. He appears to the disciples, eats fish, opens their minds to the Scriptures, promises the Spirit's power, and ascends while blessing them.
