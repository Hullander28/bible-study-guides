tagline: Walk in truth and love, and beware of deceivers
author: "The elder" (1), traditionally identified as John the apostle.
date: About AD 85-95, from Ephesus, around the same time as 1 John.
audience: "The elect lady and her children," most likely a local church and its members, or possibly a Christian woman and her family.
setting: Traveling false teachers who denied that Jesus came in the flesh relied on Christian hospitality to spread their message.
purpose: To encourage believers to keep walking in truth and love and to warn them not to support false teachers.
structure: Greeting (1-3), walk in truth and love (4-6), beware of deceivers (7-11), and closing (12-13).

- Abiding | Whoever abides in the teaching of Christ has both the Father and the Son.

## chapters
@ Truth and Love
The elder writes to the elect lady and her children, whom he loves in truth. He rejoices that some are walking in the truth and reminds them of the command from the beginning: love one another, which means walking in obedience. Many deceivers deny that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. Watch yourselves so you don't lose what you worked for. Whoever goes beyond Christ's teaching does not have God. Do not receive or greet such teachers, which would share in their wicked works. He hopes to talk face to face, so their joy may be complete.
