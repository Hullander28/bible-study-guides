tagline: Support those who work for the truth; imitate good, not evil
author: "The elder" (1), traditionally identified as John the apostle.
date: About AD 85-95, from Ephesus.
audience: Gaius, a beloved believer known for his hospitality.
setting: Traveling missionaries depended on churches for support. A domineering church leader, Diotrephes, refused to welcome them and opposed John's authority.
purpose: To commend Gaius for his faithfulness and hospitality, rebuke Diotrephes's pride, and recommend Demetrius.
structure: Commendation of Gaius (1-8), rebuke of Diotrephes (9-10), commendation of Demetrius (11-12), and closing (13-15).

## themes
- Walking in Truth | John's greatest joy is to hear his children walk in the truth.
- Hospitality | Supporting traveling believers makes us fellow workers for the truth.
- Pride in Leadership | Diotrephes "likes to put himself first."
- Imitation | "Do not imitate evil but imitate good."
- Good Testimony | Demetrius has a good testimony from everyone.

## chapters
@ Fellow Workers for the Truth
The elder prays for Gaius's health, as his soul is well. He rejoices that Gaius walks in the truth and has faithfully welcomed traveling brothers, even strangers, who went out for the sake of the Name; supporting them makes us fellow workers for the truth. But Diotrephes, who loves to be first, refuses to acknowledge John, spreads wicked talk, and puts out those who welcome the brothers. "Do not imitate evil but imitate good." Demetrius has a good testimony from everyone. John hopes to see Gaius soon.
